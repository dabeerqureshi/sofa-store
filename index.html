About us

Montreal's Local Sofa Team

Montreal Sofa Co. is a Montreal-based furniture business built on a simple promise: quality sofas, honest service, and a buying process that puts you first.

We keep a curated stock of premium sofas — from compact two-seaters for downtown condos to full sectionals for family homes — and we deliver them ourselves across the city and beyond.

No showroom pressure. No payment before you've seen your sofa in person. Just good furniture from people who stand behind it.
